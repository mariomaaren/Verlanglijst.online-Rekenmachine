# Privacy Policy

Verlanglijst.online Calculator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension reads product prices displayed on verlanglijst.online/mijnlijstjes only in the user's browser to calculate a total.

The extension saves settings such as theme, banner position, currency symbol, font size, and border preference using Chrome storage sync.

No data is sent to the developer or to any third-party server.
